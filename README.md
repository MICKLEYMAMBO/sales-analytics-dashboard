📊 Sales Dashboard — SalesRite Store Analysis
An interactive Excel dashboard built on 9,994 line items across 5,009 orders (2022–2025), combining formula-driven KPIs with a native Power Pivot data model.
Built by Mambo Mickley — Business Administration student, Assistant Sales in Charge at Paramount Commodities, and aspiring Business Intelligence & Data Analyst.
🎯 What This Project Demonstrates
This isn't a static report — it's a live, filterable decision-support tool. Change the Region, Category, Manager, or Year filter and every KPI, chart, and pivot recalculates instantly. It was built to show the full range of what an analyst is expected to deliver: clean data modeling, advanced formula work, and — most importantly — the ability to turn numbers into a business story.

🔑 Key Findings
$2.30M in total sales and $286K in profit (12.5% overall margin) across 5,009 orders, 2022–2025
Sales grew every year — a 51% increase from 2022 ($484K) to 2025 ($733K)
Technology is the strongest category (17.4% margin); Furniture is the weakest (2.5% margin) — driven by structurally unprofitable Tables and Bookcases sub-categories
West (Anna Andreadi) is the top-performing region at 14.9% margin; Central (Kelly Williams) lags at 7.9% despite solid sales volume
Overall return rate sits at 5.9%, consistent across regions — not currently a hidden problem in any one territory
Full write-up with recommended actions is in the Insight Summary tab.
🧠 Why a "Metrics Guide" Tab
Running SQL queries and building dashboards isn't enough to stand out as an analyst — you also need to speak the language of business. Every metric in this workbook is tagged to the team that owns it (Finance, Sales/Marketing, or Operations/Product) and paired with a plain-English reason it matters. That's the layer that turns a dashboard into something a hiring manager or a real stakeholder can actually use.
